One Shot

About the Edition:

  • Originally published: May 2005
  • Author: Lee Child
  • Page count: 496 (Paperback)
  • Genres: Suspense, Detective, Action

Description/Summary:

Six shots are fired in a busy public plaza and five people are killed. When police arrest an ex-military sniper, his only words are: "Get Jack Reacher for me." Reacher arrives to uncover a dangerous conspiracy.
