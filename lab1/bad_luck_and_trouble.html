Bad Luck and Trouble

About the Edition:

  • Originally published: April 2007
  • Author: Lee Child
  • Page count: 448 (Paperback)
  • Genres: Action, Military Thriller, Mystery
  • Publishers: Delacorte Press, Bantam Press

Description/Summary:

Bad Luck and Trouble is the eleventh novel in the Jack Reacher series (and the basis for Season 2 of the Amazon Prime series Reacher). From a helicopter flying high above the California desert, a man is thrown to his death.

When Frances Neagley discovers a coded deposit in Reacher's bank account, Reacher reunites with the surviving elite members of the 110th MP Special Investigations Unit. They soon discover that someone is hunting down their former teammates one by one, sparking an all-out war of vengeance and brotherhood.
