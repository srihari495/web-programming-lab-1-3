Running Blind

About the Edition:

  • Originally published: April 2000
  • Author: Lee Child
  • Page count: 528 (Paperback)
  • Genres: Psychological Thriller, Crime, Suspense
  • Publishers: Bantam Press, Putnam

Description/Summary:

Running Blind (also published as The Visitor in the UK) plunges Jack Reacher into a chilling psychological mystery. Across the United States, women with military backgrounds are being murdered in their own bathtubs without a single mark on their bodies, leaving forensic investigators completely baffled.

Every victim has one thing in common: they previously filed sexual harassment claims in the military that Jack Reacher investigated while in the MP Corps. The FBI considers Reacher either the prime suspect or their only hope to catch a calculating ghost of a serial killer.
