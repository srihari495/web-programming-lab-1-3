It ends with us

By Colleen Hoover
  • Published: August 2, 2016
  • Publisher: Atria Books
  • Pages: 384 pages
  • Genre: Romance Novel, Contemporary Fiction
  • Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.7 / 5)

Description / Synopsis:

Lily hasn't always had it easy, but that's never stopped her from working hard for the life she wants. She's come a long way from the small town where she grew up—she graduated from college, moved to Boston, and started her own floral business.

When she feels a spark with a gorgeous neurosurgeon named Ryle Kincaid, everything in Lily's life seems almost too good to be true. But as questions about her new relationship overwhelm her, so do thoughts of Atlas Corrigan—her first love and a link to the past she left behind.
