Verity

By Colleen Hoover
  • Published: December 7, 2018
  • Publisher: Grand Central Publishing
  • Pages: 336 pages
  • Genre: Psychological Thriller, Suspense Fiction
  • Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.9 / 5)

Description / Synopsis:

Lowen Ashleigh is a struggling writer on the brink of financial ruin when she accepts the job offer of a lifetime. Jeremy Crawford, husband of bestselling author Verity Crawford, has hired Lowen to complete the remaining books of a successful contract his injured wife is unable to finish.

Lowen arrives at the Crawford home, ready to sort through years of Verity's notes. What she didn't expect to uncover in the chaotic office is an unpublished autobiography that contains bone-chilling admissions about Verity's life and the dark secrets of their family.
