Description / Synopsis:

Before It Ends with Us, it started with Atlas. Colleen Hoover tells the fan-favorite Atlas's side of the story and shares what comes next in this long-anticipated sequel to the emotional worldwide bestseller.

Switching between the perspectives of Lily and Atlas, It Starts with Us picks up right where the epilogue for the groundbreaking novel left off, revealing more about Atlas's past and following Lily as she embraces a second chance at true love while navigating a jealous ex-husband.
